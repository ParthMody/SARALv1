Eligibility Criteria

Eligible if the applicant can show:

  • Structure existed on or before 1 January 2011 (with documentary proof)
  • Current occupancy confirmed
  • Self-declaration: no other property owned
  • Identity document (Aadhaar) submitted

Accepted proof of pre-2011 residence:

  • Voter roll entry before 2011
  • Electricity bill / connection before 2011
  • Property tax receipt before 2011
  • Housing society membership registered before 2011

Grounds for rejection may include:

  • Insufficient documentary proof of pre-cutoff residence
  • Prior rehabilitation received by applicant or family
  • Inability to demonstrate independent occupancy
  • Inconsistencies in self-declaration or supporting documents
