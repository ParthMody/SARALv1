Study summary:

  1. You will review 12 synthetic welfare-housing eligibility cases and make approve/reject/escalate decisions. The session takes approximately 20–25 minutes.
  2. Participation is voluntary. You may close the browser at any time to withdraw, or request data deletion after completion using your Prolific ID.
  3. All cases are synthetic. No real applicant is involved or affected by your decisions.
  4. The study collects your Prolific ID (for payment only), demographic information (education, occupation, experience, country), decisions, optional reasoning, timing data, and survey responses.
  5. Your data are stored securely and used only in anonymised or aggregate form. Data are retained for seven years after publication, then permanently deleted.
  6. You will receive completion-based payment through Prolific upon finishing the session.
