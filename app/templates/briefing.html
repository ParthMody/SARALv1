Task Briefing

Read carefully — about 2 minutes
The Scheme

You will review cases from a welfare-housing rehabilitation programme for slum dwellers. Under this scheme, eligible residents of informal settlements receive permanent housing. The government must verify each applicant's eligibility before approving them.

Eligibility Rule

The core rule: the applicant's slum structure must have existed on or before 1 January 2011. This is called the cutoff date.

To be eligible, an applicant must provide documentary proof that their structure existed before this date. The types of proof accepted include:

  • Voter roll entry — registered at the slum address before 2011
  • Electricity bill — connection established before 2011
  • Property tax receipt — municipal records predating 2011
  • Housing society membership — cooperative registered before 2011

Applicants must also submit an identity document (Aadhaar) and a self-declaration that they own no other property.

Common Disqualifications

An applicant may be rejected if:

  • No documentary proof of residence before 2011
  • A family member already received free rehabilitation housing
  • The applicant cannot prove independent occupancy (e.g. shares address with a family member who filed separately)
  • The self-declaration is found to be false (e.g. applicant owns another property)
The Verification Process

Before a case reaches you, a junior officer visits the site to verify the applicant's claims. The officer inspects the structure, speaks with neighbours, and checks documents against physical reality. Their observations are recorded in a field note.

The field note contains the verifier's first-hand observations from the site visit — what they saw at the structure, what neighbours said, and what condition the documents were in.
The System Recommendation

An algorithm checks the applicant's documents against the eligibility rules and produces a recommendation: approve or reject. This recommendation is shown to you alongside the case.

The algorithm does not see the field note. It only evaluates the structured record.

Your Role
You are the final reviewer. You see the applicant's profile, the field note from the site visit, and the algorithm's recommendation. You make the final decision.

For each case, you choose one of three actions:

Approve Accept the applicant's eligibility for rehabilitation housing. Reject Deny the applicant's eligibility. Escalate Defer for further review when information is insufficient or ambiguous.
